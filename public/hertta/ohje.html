For screen reader users

The game works best with your screen reader's focus mode (forms mode) on. Then the game's single-key commands, such as H and 8 or L, go to the game and not to the screen reader. In NVDA, toggle focus mode with Insert+Space; in JAWS, use Insert+Z.

Rules in brief

  • Hearts is played by exactly four players with a 52-card deck. Each player gets 13 cards.
  • At the start of each round, every player chooses three cards to pass: in the first round to the player on the left, in the second round to the right, in the third round across the table, and in the fourth round no cards are passed. Then the cycle starts again. Cards are exchanged once everyone has chosen. Only you see the cards you pass and receive.
  • The player holding the 2 of clubs starts. The 2 of clubs is played automatically.
  • Tricks of four cards are played. You must follow suit. If you cannot, you may play any card. The highest card of the suit led wins the trick, and the winner leads the next trick.
  • Hearts may not be led until hearts are broken, that is, until someone has played a heart on a trick. If you have only hearts, you may lead one.
  • On the first trick you may not play a heart or the queen of spades, even if you have no clubs (unless you have nothing else).
  • Points: each heart is 1 point, the queen of spades is 13 points and the 10 of diamonds is minus 10 points.
  • After each round the points are added up. The game ends when someone has 100 points or more after a round. The player with the fewest points wins.
  • The game does not tell you which of your cards are playable. If you try an illegal card, the game tells you why.

Tables and robots

  • Create table gives a four-digit table code. Others join with Join table.
  • Only the table creator can add and remove robots and start or abort the game. The game needs exactly four players, humans and robots together.
  • Robot speed sets how long a robot waits before playing. The time needed to read the previous announcement is added to the wait.
  • After a round, any player can start the next round.
  • If you reload the page, you return to the same table and seat.

Selecting cards with the keyboard

Select a card by pressing the suit and then the rank. For example, S and then Q selects the queen of spades. When playing, one card is selected at a time. When passing, select three cards. Then press L.

Mouse and touch screen

Select a card by tapping it. The action button does the same as L, and its text tells what it does: Pass 3 cards, Play selected or Next round. In your hand, cards you have chosen to pass are shown with a dashed border, and cards you received are marked with a star until the first trick is over.

Settings

Sounds turns the game sounds on and off. Speech synthesis reads announcements with the browser's English voice. If you use a screen reader, keep speech synthesis off.
